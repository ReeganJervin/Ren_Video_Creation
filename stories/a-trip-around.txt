title: A Trip Around
place: meadow
time: day

# --- The meadow ---
ren enters from left
+ yuki enters from right
+ narrator: One morning, Ren and Yuki set off on an adventure.
yuki (happy): Where shall we go first?
ren (playful): Everywhere!

# --- The forest ---
place: forest
narrator: First, they explored the shady forest.
yuki looks up
yuki (surprised): Look at the sunbeams!

# --- The river ---
place: river
narrator: Next, they found a sparkling river.
ren (happy): Let's cross the little bridge!
yuki (shy): The water looks so cold...

# --- The snowy hill ---
place: snowy hill
narrator: Then up a snowy hill they went.
yuki hops twice
yuki (playful): I love the snow!
ren (surprised): Look, a snowman!

# --- The beach ---
place: beach
time: sunset
narrator: At last, they reached the beach as the sun went down.
ren and yuki hop
yuki hugs ren
+ yuki (sleepy): What a wonderful trip...
ren (happy): Let's go again tomorrow!
ren and yuki wave
