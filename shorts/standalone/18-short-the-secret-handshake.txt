title: The Secret Handshake
type: short
hook: Ren invented a SECRET handshake
thumbnail text: Secret Handshake
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: day
music: happy

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
ren (excited): Yuki! I invented a secret explorer handshake!
yuki (happy): Show me!
ren (proud): First, paws together.
ren holds out his paw to yuki
yuki touches paws with ren
ren (proud): Then two hops.
ren and yuki hop
ren (proud): Then a spin, a wiggle, a very serious nod...
ren dances
ren (excited): ...and then the secret word!
yuki (surprised): What's the secret word?
beat
shot: close on ren
ren (thinking): I forgot the secret word.
shot: medium on both
yuki (laughing): Hehe! Ren!
ren (pouty): It was a very secret word. Even from me.
yuki walks to ren
yuki hugs ren
yuki (shy): How about a hug instead?
hearts float up
ren (shy): ...That's a much better handshake.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (playful): And no secret word.
