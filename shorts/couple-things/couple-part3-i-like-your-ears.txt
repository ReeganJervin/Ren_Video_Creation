title: I Like Your Ears
type: short
hook: Part 3: The day he tried to say it
thumbnail text: "I Like Your... Ears?"
mood voices: on
place: river
time: sunset
music: calm

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
shot: medium on both
yuki (happy): These berries are so good.
ren (thinks silently): Today is the day. Just say it, Ren.
ren (shy): Yuki, I have something important to tell you.
yuki (surprised): What is it?
music: heartfelt
shot: close on ren
ren (shy): I... I like your ears.
shot: medium on both
yuki (surprised): My ears?
ren (worried): And your laugh. And how you're always right. I mean...
ren (shy): I think I really, really like you.
beat
yuki walks to ren
yuki (shy): Ren. I love you.
shot: close on ren
ren (shouts, surprised): Wait! Did you just say it first?
shot: medium on both
yuki (playful): I did.
ren hugs yuki
hearts float up
ren (thinks silently): I'm still saying I said it first.
ending: And that's how it all began. Follow!
