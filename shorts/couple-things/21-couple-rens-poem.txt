title: Ren's Poem
type: short
hook: He wrote you a love poem...
thumbnail text: Ren's Love Poem
mood voices: on
place: cottage garden
time: sunset
music: calm

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
shot: medium on both
ren (shy): Yuki, I wrote you a poem.
yuki (excited): A poem? For me?
ren writes in the air
ren (proud): Roses are red. Foxes are orange.
ren (thinking): Nothing rhymes with orange.
yuki (laughing): That's the poem?
ren (determined): Wait. There's more.
music: heartfelt
shot: close on ren
ren (shy): I'm not good with words. But every day with you is my favourite day.
shot: medium on both
yuki walks to ren
yuki (shy): That's the best poem I've ever heard.
ren (shy): Even the orange part?
yuki (playful): Especially the orange part.
yuki hugs ren
ending: Tag the one who'd write you a bad poem!
