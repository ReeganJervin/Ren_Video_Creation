title: Where Should We Eat?
type: short
hook: POV: you ask her where to eat
thumbnail text: "Anything Is Fine"
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: sunset
music: happy
thumbnail: 16.6

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
ren (happy): I'm so hungry. Where should we eat?
yuki (happy): Anything is fine.
ren (excited): Pizza?
yuki (pouty): Not pizza.
ren (thinking): Noodles?
yuki shakes its head
yuki: We had noodles last week.
ren (worried): Burgers?
yuki (pouty): Too heavy.
ren (thinks silently): "Anything is fine," she said.
ren (determined): Okay. You choose.
yuki (thinking): Hmm...
beat
yuki (excited): Pizza!
shot: close on ren
ren (shouts, surprised): I said pizza first!
yuki (playful): But now I want it.
shot: medium on both
ren hugs yuki
ren (laughing): Pizza it is.
ending: Tag the one who says "anything"!
