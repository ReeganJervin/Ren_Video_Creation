title: The Secret Map
type: short
hook: Ren found a TREASURE MAP!
thumbnail text: Treasure Map!
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: day
music: adventure

ren enters from left to left
ren finds the map
ren picks up the map
ren opens the map
shot: close on ren
ren (excited): A treasure map! A real one! With an X!
ren holds up the map
ren (determined): Treasure, here I come!
ren runs to center
ren runs to right
ren looks around
ren (thinking): The X is right here. But where is the treasure?
ren puts down the map
yuki enters from right to right
shot: medium on both
yuki (shy): Surprise.
ren (surprised): Yuki? You're the treasure?
yuki (shy): I made the map. You looked a little sad yesterday.
yuki holds the cookie
yuki gives the cookie to ren
music: heartfelt
ren (shy): You drew a whole map... just to cheer me up?
yuki (playful): And a cookie. The map was the hard part.
hearts float up
ren (happy): Best treasure ever.
