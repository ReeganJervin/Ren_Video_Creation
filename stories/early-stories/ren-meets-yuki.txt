title: Ren Meets Yuki
place: meadow
time: day

# --- Ren is all alone ---
ren enters from left to left
+ narrator: One sunny morning, Ren was all alone.
ren (sad): I wish I had a friend...

# --- A bunny hops in ---
yuki enters from right to right
+ narrator: Then a little bunny came hopping by.
ren turns to yuki
ren (surprised): Oh! Hello!
yuki (surprised): Eek! A fox!
yuki (shy): Um... I'm Yuki.

# --- Making friends (close-up on both) ---
ren walks to yuki
camera: close
ren (happy): I'm Ren! Will you be my friend?
yuki looks at ren
yuki (happy): Yes, please!
camera: wide

# --- Playing together ---
ren (playful): Bet you can't hop as high as me!
yuki (playful): Watch me!
yuki hops twice
ren hops
yuki (pouty): Hmph! You hopped higher!
ren (happy): Then let's hop together!
ren and yuki hop twice

# --- A hug (closer) ---
camera: closer
yuki hugs ren
+ narrator: And that was the start of a wonderful friendship.
camera: wide

# --- Evening ---
time: sunset
yuki (sad): The sun is going down...
yuki (sleepy): And I'm so sleepy.
ren (shy): Play again tomorrow?
yuki (happy): Every day, Ren!
ren and yuki wave
