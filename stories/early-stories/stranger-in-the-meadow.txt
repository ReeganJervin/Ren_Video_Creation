title: A Stranger in the Meadow
place: meadow
time: sunset

# --- Scene 1: Someone New ---

narrator: The next day, Ren returned to the meadow with a brand-new plan.
ren enters from left to left
ren (happy): Today's the day!
ren (playful): New adventure, new mystery, and absolutely no suspicious leaves!
ren (happy): And maybe, just maybe, I'll meet someone interesting.
ren looks around
ren (shy): Someone to talk to would be nice, too.
narrator: Ren didn't know that, on the other side of the meadow, someone else was having a very similar day.
yuki enters from right to right
yuki (shy): Hello? Is anybody here?
wait 1
yuki (sad): I guess not.
ren (surprised): Huh?
ren looks around
ren (surprised): Who said that?
yuki (surprised): Who said that?
ren (shy): I did!
yuki (shy): So did I!
wait 1
ren (surprised): Oh!
yuki (surprised): Oh!
ren (playful): Either this meadow has an echo...
yuki (playful): ...or we're both very confused.
ren (happy): I'm going with confused. It's usually safer.

# --- Scene 2: The Awkward Introduction ---

ren looks at yuki
ren (shy): Um... hello. I'm Ren.
yuki looks at ren
yuki (shy): Hi. I'm Yuki.
ren (happy): Nice to meet you, Yuki!
yuki (happy): Nice to meet you too, Ren.
wait 1
ren (playful): So... do you come here often?
yuki (surprised): It's a meadow.
ren (surprised): Right.
ren (playful): Excellent observation. You're already a better explorer than me.
yuki (playful): I haven't explored anything yet.
ren (happy): Then we're tied!
yuki (happy): I suppose we are.
narrator: Both of them smiled, but neither quite knew what to say next.
ren (shy): I was just... exploring.
yuki (shy): Me too.
ren (happy): Looking for mysteries?
yuki (surprised): Mysteries?
ren (happy): Strange clues, secret places, things nobody has ever discovered!
yuki (playful): I was mostly looking for somewhere to sit.
wait 1
ren (surprised): Oh.
ren (playful): Well, sitting is a very mysterious activity.
yuki (happy): Especially when you don't know where to sit!
ren (happy): See? You're already good at this.

# --- Scene 3: Two Lonely Explorers ---

narrator: As they talked, the awkward silence slowly began to disappear.
yuki (shy): Can I ask you something, Ren?
ren (happy): Anything! Unless it's about taxes. I don't understand taxes.
yuki (surprised): What's taxes?
ren (playful): I don't know. That's why I said not to ask!
yuki (happy): You're silly.
ren (happy): Thank you. I practice every day.
yuki (shy): Do you always explore by yourself?
ren (happy): Pretty much! I discover mysteries, solve puzzles, and sometimes talk to suspicious leaves.
yuki (surprised): You talk to leaves?
ren (shy): Only when they're acting suspicious.
yuki (happy): And do they answer?
ren (playful): No. Terrible conversationalists.
wait 1
yuki (sad): I explore by myself too.
ren (surprised): Really?
yuki (sad): I look for interesting places, but I don't really have anyone to show them to.
ren (sad): I know exactly how that feels.
yuki (shy): Sometimes I imagine what I'd say if I had a friend with me.
ren (shy): I do that too.
yuki (surprised): You do?
ren (happy): Of course! Sometimes I even make up their answers.
ren (playful): Usually, they're much smarter than me.
yuki (happy): That must be a very interesting conversation.
ren (playful): It is! I win every argument.
yuki (happy): That's because you're arguing with yourself!
ren (surprised): ...You've discovered my secret.
narrator: For the first time in a long while, Ren laughed with someone instead of laughing alone.
ren (happy): Yuki, I think you might be the first person who understands my jokes.
yuki (playful): I wouldn't go that far.
ren (surprised): Ouch! My first friendship and already I'm being roasted!
yuki (happy): Maybe you'll survive.

# --- Scene 4: A Little Trust ---

ren looks at yuki
ren (shy): Can I tell you something?
yuki (happy): Sure.
ren (sad): Sometimes I find something amazing, and I turn around to show somebody...
ren (sad): ...but there's nobody there.
yuki (sad): I know that feeling.
ren (shy): I used to think that if I became a great explorer, I wouldn't feel lonely anymore.
ren (sad): But even the biggest adventure feels a little small when you're alone.
yuki (sad): I think that's why I keep coming back here.
ren (surprised): To the meadow?
yuki (shy): I guess I was hoping I'd meet someone.
wait 1
ren (surprised): You were looking for someone too?
yuki (happy): Maybe I was.
ren (happy): Then I'm really glad I came back today.
yuki (happy): Me too.
narrator: The meadow hadn't changed. The sky was still wide, the grass still stretched into the distance, and the world was still full of mysteries.
narrator: But suddenly, it didn't feel quite so enormous.
ren (playful): So, Yuki... important explorer question.
yuki (surprised): What is it?
ren (happy): If we found a mountain made of snacks, would you help me investigate it?
yuki (happy): Only if we share the snacks.
ren (surprised): Share them?
ren (playful): I was hoping you wouldn't notice that detail.
yuki (happy): Too late!
ren (happy): Fine. Fifty-fifty!
yuki (happy): Deal!

# --- Scene 5: Tomorrow, Together ---

narrator: The sun sank lower as Ren and Yuki continued talking.
ren (happy): You know, I had a whole adventure planned for today.
yuki (surprised): What kind of adventure?
ren (playful): A mystery involving tiny footprints, suspicious leaves, and possibly a monster.
yuki (surprised): A monster?
ren (shy): It might have been a leaf. The evidence was inconclusive.
yuki (happy): That's the silliest mystery I've ever heard.
ren (happy): Thank you! I've been working on it.
yuki (happy): Maybe next time we can find a real mystery.
ren (surprised): Next time?
yuki (shy): Well... if you want to explore together again.
ren (happy): Do I want to? Yuki, I have been waiting my entire life to ask someone that!
ren (playful): I mean, not you specifically. We only just met. That would be strange.
yuki (happy): A little strange.
ren (shy): Sorry. I get excited.
yuki (happy): I like excited.
wait 1
ren (happy): Then it's a promise! Tomorrow, we explore together!
yuki (happy): Tomorrow, together.
ren (playful): And the day after that?
yuki (happy): Together.
ren (happy): And the day after that?
yuki (playful): Ren, are you going to ask me this every day?
ren (happy): Probably!
yuki (happy): Then I'll answer every day.
narrator: Ren had started the morning hoping to find a mystery. Instead, he had found something much better.
narrator: He had found a friend.
ren (happy): Good night, Yuki. See you tomorrow!
yuki (happy): See you tomorrow, Ren!
narrator: And as the sunset painted the meadow gold, two little explorers went home already looking forward to their next adventure.
narrator: Their world was still full of mysteries. But now, they wouldn't have to solve them alone.
