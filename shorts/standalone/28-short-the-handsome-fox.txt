title: The Handsome Fox
type: short
hook: Who's that in the river?
thumbnail text: Who's THAT?
mood voices: on
place: river
time: day
music: happy

shot: close on ren
ren looks down
+ ren (surprised): Yuki! There's a very handsome fox in the river!
shot: medium on both
yuki (thinking): Ren. That's you.
ren (proud): I knew it.
ren (shy): Hello, handsome.
yuki looks down
yuki (happy): Look, he has a friend. A beautiful bunny.
ren (thinking): The bunny is okay.
yuki (pouty): Okay?
shot: close on ren
ren (shouts, scared): The most beautiful bunny in the whole river!
shot: close on yuki
yuki (playful): I'm the only bunny in the river.
shot: close on ren
ren (proud): Still counts.
