hook: The scariest question in a relationship

mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: day
music: happy

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
yuki (excited): Ren! Do you know what day it is today?
music: tense
shot: close on ren
pop: GULP! near ren
ren (thinks silently): Oh no. Oh no no no.
ren (thinks): Her birthday? No, that's in May.
ren (thinks): The day we met? That was in spring... It IS spring.
ren (worried): Of course I know. It's a very special day.
yuki (happy): It is!
ren (scared): The most special day... of the whole year!
shot: medium on both
music: happy

beat
ren (laughing): Pancakes. Of course. I knew that.
yuki (playful): You didn't know.
ren (shy): I did not know.
hearts float up
ending: Tag the one who panics at this question!
