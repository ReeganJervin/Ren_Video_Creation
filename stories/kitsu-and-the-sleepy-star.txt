title: Kitsu and the Sleepy Star
place: meadow
time: day

# --- Beginning: a happy day ---
kitsu enters from right
+ narrator: One bright afternoon, Kitsu skipped through the meadow.
kitsu turns to you
title card: KITSU
kitsu (happy): What a perfect day for an adventure!
kitsu hops

# --- Evening comes ---
time: sunset
narrator: The sun slowly began to set, painting the sky orange.
kitsu walks to left
kitsu looks up
star appears
kitsu (surprised): Oh! The first star of the evening!
star twinkles

# --- The star gets sleepy ---
time: dusk
star falls
kitsu feels sad
kitsu (sad): Oh no... little star, are you hurt?
kitsu picks up the star
camera: close
star glows
+ kitsu (shy): It's okay. I'm right here with you.

# --- Sending it home ---
time: night
camera: wide
kitsu walks to center
kitsu feels happy
kitsu lets go of the star
+ narrator: With a gentle push, Kitsu sent the star back home.
kitsu waves
star twinkles
heart floats down
kitsu hops twice
hearts float up

# --- Goodnight ---
kitsu (sleepy): Goodnight, little star...
narrator: And from that night on, the star always shone a little brighter for Kitsu.
