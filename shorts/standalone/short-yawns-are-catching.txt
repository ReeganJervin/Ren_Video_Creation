title: Yawns Are Catching
type: short
hook: Try not to yawn...
thumbnail text: Don't Yawn!
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: night
music: bedtime

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
ren (determined): Tonight, we stay up and watch every single star.
yuki (determined): Every single one.
yuki yawns
shot: close on ren
ren (pouty): Yuki! No yawning! Yawns are catching!
ren yawns
shot: medium on both
yuki (playful): You yawned!
ren (sleepy): That wasn't a yawn. That was a... very big breath.
owl flies in from right
owl: Hoo. What are you two doing up so late?
ren (proud): Staying awake. We're professionals.
owl (sleepy): Hoo... aaah.
yuki yawns
+ ren yawns
beat
ren (sleepy): Mister Owl. You just made it worse.
ren and yuki sit down
ren and yuki feel sleepy
owl (sleepy): Goodnight, little professionals.
narrator: They watched exactly three stars.
