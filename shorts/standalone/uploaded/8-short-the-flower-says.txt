title: The Flower Says
type: short
hook: The flower says... NOT brave?!
thumbnail text: The Flower Says...
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: sunset
music: calm

shot: close on ren
ren holds the flower
+ ren (whispers): Brave.
ren (whispers, worried): Not brave.
ren (whispers): Brave.
ren (whispers, worried): Not brave...
beat
ren (sad): Last petal. The flower says not brave.
yuki enters from right to right
yuki (playful): Who's not brave?
ren jumps in fright
camera: shake
ren (shouts, scared): Nobody! The flower! The flower is not brave!
shot: medium on both
yuki (thinking): Ren. You walked into the dark forest with me. Twice.
ren (shy): Only because you were there.
yuki (happy): You're the bravest fox I know.
ren feels happy
hearts float up
ren (proud): Hear that, flower?
shot: close on yuki
yuki (playful): Also the only fox I know.
