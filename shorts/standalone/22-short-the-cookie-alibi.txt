# ============================================================
#  Ren & Yuki Short - The Cookie Alibi   (~30 s, vertical)
#  Did Ren eat Yuki's cookie? Every excuse is worse than the last.
#  In The Compliment's and Very Local Wind's style: the first line is the
#  joke (no walking in), one joke that grows, a last line that loops.
#
#  Commands (run them in the project folder, or drag the story onto the .bat file):
#    Check it:            check-story.bat shorts\standalone\22-short-the-cookie-alibi.txt
#    Watch it:            preview.bat shorts\standalone\22-short-the-cookie-alibi.txt
#    Make the video:      make-video.bat shorts\standalone\22-short-the-cookie-alibi.txt
#    If it stopped:       resume-video.bat shorts\standalone\22-short-the-cookie-alibi.txt
#    YouTube files only:  node tools\make-video.js shorts\standalone\22-short-the-cookie-alibi.txt --youtube
#    Instagram files:     make-social.bat shorts\standalone\22-short-the-cookie-alibi.txt
# ============================================================

title: The Cookie Alibi
type: short
hook: Did Ren eat her cookie?
thumbnail text: Not Me!
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: day
music: happy

shot: close on yuki
yuki (pouty): Ren. Did you eat my cookie?
shot: close on ren
ren holds the cookie
+ ren (proud): No.
shot: medium on both
yuki (thinking): You're holding it.
ren (shy): This is a different cookie.
yuki (playful): It has a bite out of it.
ren (thinking): It came like that. Cookies are born that way.
yuki (playful): You have crumbs on your nose.
ren (whispers, shy): ...Those are my freckles.
beat
yuki (pouty): Ren.
ren (sad): Okay. I ate half.
ren gives the cookie to yuki
ren (shy): But I saved you the big half.
hearts float up
shot: close on yuki
yuki (playful): It's the small half.
