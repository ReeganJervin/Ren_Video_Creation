title: Jealous of a Butterfly
type: short
hook: When he gets jealous of... a butterfly
thumbnail text: Jealous of a BUTTERFLY?
mood voices: on
place: cottage garden
time: day
music: happy

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
shot: medium on both
yuki (happy): Look at that butterfly. It's so pretty.
ren (pouty): Prettier than me?
yuki (playful): It has very pretty wings.
ren (determined): I have very pretty ears.
ren dances
ren (proud): And I can dance. Can a butterfly dance?
yuki (thinking): Butterflies dance all day.
shot: close on ren
ren (shouts, pouty): Show-offs!
shot: medium on both
yuki walks to ren
yuki (shy): Ren. You're the prettiest fox I know.
ren (shy): ...Prettier than the butterfly?
yuki (playful): Don't push it.
ren hugs yuki
ending: Tag the one who gets jealous of anything!
