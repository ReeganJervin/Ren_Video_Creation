6. GOOD TO KNOW
---------------
- "Made for Kids": YouTube asks this for the channel and for every video. It's your choice; the engine never
  decides it for you. Videos for kids and adults together are often set to "No", but read YouTube's guidance first.
- Channel trailer (Customisation > Layout): once Episode 1 is out, a 30–60 s Short or a cut from Episode 1 works well.
- Every video already comes with its own thumbnail, subtitles, description draft and chapters (in its folder in videos\).
