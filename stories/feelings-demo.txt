title: Feelings
place: meadow
time: day
music: happy
mood voices: on

scene: The Cake
ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
yuki (excited): Ren! I baked a cake for our picnic!
ren (surprised): A whole cake? Just for us?
yuki (proud): Three layers. With strawberries on top.
ren (thinks): Maybe she won't notice.
yuki (thinks silently): Why is there a slice missing?
yuki (thinking): Ren. Where is the fourth slice?
ren (shy): Um. Quality control?
yuki (pouty): Ren!
ren (playful): It was very good quality.
yuki (laughing): You are impossible!
ren feels laughing
wait 1
yuki (whispers): Next time, I'm hiding it.
ren (shouts, excited): I heard that!
yuki (surprised): You have very big ears.
ren (proud): The biggest in the whole meadow.

scene: The Storm
time: night
music: deep tense
narrator: That night, a storm rolled over the meadow.
ren and yuki feel scared
yuki (scared): Ren? Where are you?
ren (shouts, worried): Yuki! Over here!
yuki (crying): I thought I lost you.
music: deep sad
ren (sad): I'm here. I'm not going anywhere.
ren walks to yuki
ren hugs yuki
music: heartfelt
yuki (determined): Then we wait it out. Together.
ren (worried): What if the thunder comes back?
yuki (whispers): Then I will hold your paw.
ren (sleepy): Together. And maybe a little nap.
yuki (happy): Goodnight, Ren.
music: epic
time: day
narrator: By morning the storm had passed, and a whole new adventure was waiting.
