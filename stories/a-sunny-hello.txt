# A short daytime story that shows Ren's moods.

title: A Sunny Hello
place: meadow
time: day

ren enters from right
+ narrator: It was a bright and sunny morning in the meadow.
ren waves
+ ren (happy): Hello, friends! I'm Ren!
camera: close
ren (shy): Will you... play with me today?
ren feels sad
narrator: But nobody answered, and Ren felt a little lonely.
ren looks around
sparkles
ren (surprised): Oh! You're here!
ren feels happy
ren hops twice
hearts float up
camera: wide
time: sunset
narrator: They played all day, until the sun went down.
ren (sleepy): What a wonderful day...
ren exits to left
