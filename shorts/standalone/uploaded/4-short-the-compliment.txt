title: The Compliment
type: short
hook: Ren practises a compliment...
thumbnail text: Second-Best?!
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: sunset
music: calm

ren enters from left to left
ren (whispers): Okay, Ren. Just say it. Yuki, your ears are very nice.
ren (thinking): No. Too simple.
ren (whispers): Yuki, your ears are very... tall.
ren (proud): Yuki, your ears are the second-best ears in the whole meadow.
beat
ren (surprised): Wait. Who has the best ears?
ren turns to you
ren (proud): Me. Obviously.
yuki enters from right to right
yuki (pouty): Second-best?
camera: shake
shot: close on ren
ren (shouts, scared): First! First-best! Tied first! Very tied!
shot: medium on both
yuki (laughing): Hehe. Your tail is very fluffy.
ren feels shy
hearts float up
shot: close on ren
ren (shy): ...It's the best tail in the meadow.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (playful): Second-best.
