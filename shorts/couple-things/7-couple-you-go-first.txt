title: You Go First
type: short
hook: Couples saying goodbye be like...
thumbnail text: No, YOU Go First
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: night
music: calm
shot: medium on both

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
yuki (sleepy): It's so late. I should go home.
ren (happy): Okay. Good night!
yuki (shy): Good night.
beat
ren and yuki look at each other
ren (playful): You go first.
yuki (playful): No, you go first.
ren (shy): I can't leave first. You go.
yuki (pouty): Then we'll stand here forever.
ren (proud): Fine by me.
yuki walks to ren
yuki (laughing): You're so silly.
ren sits down
+ yuki sits down
narrator: Three hours later...
time: day
ren feels sleepy
+ yuki feels sleepy
ren (sleepy): ...You go first.
yuki (sleepy): ...No, you.
ending: Tag the one who never says bye first!
