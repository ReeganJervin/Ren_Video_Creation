title: Sorry, Yuki
place: beach
time: day

# --- A sunny race ---
ren enters from left to left
+ narrator: Ren loved spending sunny days by the sea.
yuki enters from right to right
yuki (happy): Ren! Let's race to the waves!
ren turns to yuki
ren (playful): Okay! Ready... set... go!
ren and yuki hop
ren hops twice
ren (happy): I win! I'm the fastest fox in the world!

# --- Yuki gets upset ---
yuki feels pouty
yuki (pouty): Hmph! You started before I said go!
ren (playful): Did not!
yuki (sad): You always win. It's not fun anymore.
yuki exits to right
narrator: Yuki hopped away, and the beach suddenly felt very quiet.

# --- Ren feels bad ---
ren feels sad
ren looks around
camera: close
ren (sad): Oh no... I wasn't being a good friend.
wait 1
camera: wide

# --- Saying sorry ---
time: sunset
narrator: As the sun began to set, Ren went to find Yuki.
ren walks to center
yuki enters from right
yuki feels sad
ren walks to yuki
ren (shy): Yuki... I'm sorry. I really did start too early.
yuki looks at ren
yuki (surprised): You're saying sorry?
ren (happy): Winning isn't fun without you. Next time, you count!
yuki feels happy
yuki (playful): Hehe! Okay, but I'll win this time!
title card: FRIENDS
ren and yuki hug

# --- A happy ending ---
time: dusk
narrator: They raced until the stars came out, and nobody kept score.
ren and yuki hop
time: night
camera: close
star appears
ren looks at the star
+ yuki looks at the star
star twinkles
yuki (sleepy): Best day ever...
ren (happy): Best friends ever.
ren and yuki wave
