# ============================================================
#  Ren & Yuki Short - The Echo   (~40 s, vertical)
#  Ren talks to the "echo" by the river. The echo has opinions.
#  Uses: river, Ren, Yuki (hiding behind Ren), whispers, shouts.
# ============================================================

title: The Echo
type: short
hook: This echo talks back...
thumbnail text: Talking Echo?!
mood voices: on
place: river
time: day
music: calm

ren enters from left to center
+ yuki enters from left to left
yuki hides behind ren
ren (shouts, happy): Hellooo!
yuki (whispers, playful): Hellooo...
ren (surprised): An echo! A real echo!
ren (shouts, proud): I am the bravest fox in the meadow!
yuki (whispers, playful): ...second bravest...
shot: close on ren
ren (surprised): What?
ren (shouts, determined): The BRAVEST!
yuki (whispers, playful): ...the bunny is braver...
ren (pouty): This echo is very rude.
music: tense
ren (shouts, thinking): Echo! Who's there?
beat
yuki peeks out
shot: medium on both
yuki (laughing): It's me!
ren jumps in fright
ren (shouts, surprised): Yuki!
music: happy
ren (pouty): How long have you been there?
yuki (playful): Since "Hellooo".
ren (shy): So you heard the bravest fox part.
yuki (playful): The second bravest fox.
