#  Uses ONLY: the meadow, Ren, Yuki, times of day, music, camera.
#  Saved for later episodes: the star, the owl, the cottage, the
#  lantern and every other place.
#
#  The idea: a "mock epic". Ren plans a legendary expedition to the
#  top of a tiny hill, forty steps away. When he finally gets there,
#  someone is already sitting at the top.
# ============================================================

title: The Beginning of a Friendship
editing: auto


# ================= Scene 1 - The hook (about 30 s) =================
scene: The Greatest Expedition
place: meadow
time: day
music: adventure

narrator: Every great explorer remembers their first expedition.
narrator: Some cross wild oceans. Some climb the tallest mountains in the world.
ren enters from left to left
camera: push in
ren (happy): Today, I conquer... the summit!
camera: shake
shot: wide
narrator: The summit was a small hill in the middle of the meadow. It was about forty steps away.
beat
title card: REN AND YUKI


# ================= Scene 2 - The fox with a plan =================
scene: The Fox Who Had a Plan
music: happy

ren turns to you
ren (happy): Good morning, world! Ren is here, and today is going to be legendary!
beat
ren (playful): Please hold your applause until I reach the top.
ren looks at you
ren (surprised): You're thinking: Ren, it's just a little hill.
ren (shy): That's exactly what the hill wants you to think.
narrator: Ren was a little fox with a very big imagination. Unfortunately, it was much better at making plans than following them.
ren (happy): Explorer's rule number one: always look confident.
ren (playful): Even when you have absolutely no idea what you're doing.
ren (happy): Rule number two: if something goes wrong, call it an experiment.
ren (surprised): And rule number three...
beat
ren (sad): Always bring a snack. Which I did not.
ren looks right
ren (surprised): Now. Which is the best way up?
ren looks left
ren (playful): Left looks adventurous.
ren looks right
ren (surprised): But right looks even more adventurous.
shot: close on ren
ren (shy): And straight ahead looks suspiciously easy.
ren (playful): Every explorer knows the easy way is a trap.
beat
ren (happy): So I'll take the easy way. Because I'm very brave.
ren hops twice
ren (playful): Brilliant decision, Ren. Truly brilliant.


# ================= Scene 3 - A little lonely =================
scene: Nobody to Tell
music: calm

ren walks to center slowly
narrator: But halfway up the very small mountain, Ren stopped.
ren looks around
ren (happy): If somebody were here, I'd say: behold, the magnificent meadow!
ren (happy): And they'd say: wow, Ren, you're amazing!
ren (shy): And I'd say: oh, stop it. You're making me blush.
beat
ren feels sad
ren (sad): Although... I suppose I'd have to find a somebody first.
narrator: Ren loved imagining adventures with a friend. But imagination couldn't laugh at his jokes, or walk beside him.
ren (shy): Maybe one day I'll meet someone who likes adventures, silly jokes, and very important snack breaks.
ren feels happy
ren (happy): But a legendary explorer never gives up!
ren (playful): Unless lunch is ready. Even legendary explorers have limits.
ren walks to left
narrator: So Ren went home for lunch, to prepare for the final climb.
ren exits to left


# ================= Scene 4 - The bunny on the hill =================
scene: The Hill of Extremely Important Sitting
music: calm

yuki enters from right to center
+ narrator: What Ren didn't know was that the hill already belonged to someone.
yuki (happy): Hello, hill. It's just you and me again.
yuki looks around
yuki (playful): You're a very good listener. You never interrupt, and you never borrow my snacks.
beat
yuki (surprised): Wait. I don't have any snacks either.
yuki (sad): Somehow, that makes this conversation worse.
narrator: Yuki was a thoughtful little bunny. She came to this hill every day, and she had given it a very important name.
yuki turns to you
yuki (happy): Welcome to the Hill of Extremely Important Sitting.
yuki (playful): It's extremely important. And there's a lot of sitting.
yuki feels sad
yuki (sad): It would be nicer with someone to sit with. Someone who wouldn't mind if I'm quiet sometimes.
narrator: Yuki didn't need someone to make her interesting. She simply wished she had someone to share it with.
yuki feels normal
yuki (surprised): Though if someone ever came, would I even remember how to say hello?
yuki (playful): I should practise. Just in case.
yuki (happy): Hello!
beat
yuki (shy): That was easy.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (surprised): Oh no. I just practised saying hello to a hill.
yuki (playful): Nobody needs to know about that.
yuki looks right


# ================= Scene 5 - The final climb =================
scene: The Final Climb
time: sunset
music: tense

narrator: That evening, the legendary explorer returned for the final climb.
ren enters from left to left
ren (happy): The Grand and Glorious Expedition of Ren the Remarkable!
ren (playful): Hm. Maybe that's a little too modest.
camera: follow ren
ren walks to center slowly
+ narrator: The wind was strong. The grass was tall. The snack situation was still very serious.
ren (surprised): The air is getting thinner up here!
narrator: It wasn't.
slow motion 3
ren hops
ren (happy): I made it! The first explorer ever to reach the summit!
yuki (surprised): Huh? Who's talking?
ren (surprised): Huh? Who's talking?
reveal: yuki
ren and yuki look at each other
music: happy


# ================= Scene 6 - The most awkward hello =================
scene: The Most Awkward Hello

shot: wide
ren (surprised): Oh! Hello!
yuki (surprised): Hello!
narrator: Yuki had practised this. It did not help at all.
ren (shy): I'm Ren.
yuki (shy): I'm Yuki.
ren (playful): So... do you come to this summit often?
yuki (pouty): Summit? This is the Hill of Extremely Important Sitting.
ren (pouty): The what?
yuki (pouty): I come here every day. It's my hill.
ren (pouty): Well, I conquered it. Just now. Very bravely.
yuki (playful): You took the long way up.
ren (pouty): That was an experiment!
beat
yuki (playful): Did the experiment include going home for lunch?
ren (surprised): You saw that?
yuki (happy): The whole meadow saw that.
ren (shy): I try to be honest whenever pretending to be impressive stops working.
yuki (happy): Does that happen often?
ren (playful): More often than I'd like to admit.
ren and yuki feel happy
narrator: And just like that, the argument about the hill was over. Neither of them could remember who had won.


# ================= Scene 7 - Someone who understands =================
scene: Someone Who Understands
music: calm

ren looks around
ren (shy): Can I tell you a secret?
yuki (happy): Of course.
ren (shy): Sometimes, when I find something amazing, I turn around to show someone...
ren feels sad
ren (sad): ...and there's nobody there.
yuki looks at ren
yuki (sad): I do that too.
ren (surprised): You do?
yuki (shy): I even practise saying hello. To a hill.
beat
ren (sad): I thought I was the only one.
yuki (shy): Me too.
camera: push in
narrator: Neither of them had found a treasure that day. They had found something rarer: someone who understood a feeling they had kept to themselves.
time: dusk
ren and yuki look up
narrator: So they sat on the hill together, and watched the sun go down.
yuki (happy): You know, the view is better with two.
ren (happy): Explorer's rule number four.
yuki (surprised): There's a rule number four?
ren (shy): There is now.


# ================= Scene 8 - Same time tomorrow? =================
scene: Same Time Tomorrow?
music: happy

ren (shy): Yuki, can I ask you something?
yuki (happy): Sure!
ren (shy): Would you like to explore with me tomorrow?
beat
yuki (surprised): Tomorrow?
ren (playful): I should warn you that my plans are sometimes a little confusing.
yuki (playful): Sometimes?
ren (surprised): You're getting very good at this.
yuki (happy): I'd love to.
ren hops twice
ren (happy): Excellent! Then I officially appoint you my exploring partner!
yuki (surprised): Do I get a say in this appointment?
ren (shy): Of course. It's a very democratic appointment.
yuki (playful): Then I have one condition. Sometimes, I choose where we go.
ren (surprised): But what if you choose somewhere very confusing?
yuki (happy): Then we'll get confused together.
beat
ren (happy): Deal!
ren (playful): And one day, we'll find a mountain made entirely of cookies.
yuki (surprised): A what?
ren (happy): It's my top priority. You'll understand when you're more experienced.
yuki (playful): And when we find it, we're sharing.
ren (surprised): I should have read the friendship rules more carefully.
ren and yuki hop


# ================= Ending =================
time: night
music: bedtime
narrator: That night, the meadow was the same meadow, and the hill was the same little hill.
narrator: But for Ren and Yuki, the world felt a lot bigger.
ren looks left
+ yuki looks left
ren (surprised): Yuki... what's beyond those trees?
yuki (shy): Nobody knows. Nobody's ever been brave enough to look.
ren and yuki look at each other
ren (happy): Then I know where we're going tomorrow.
ren (happy): See you tomorrow, Yuki!
yuki (happy): See you tomorrow, Ren!
ren and yuki wave
narrator: Because sometimes, the greatest adventure begins when you meet someone who makes you want to explore the world together.
title card: THE BEGINNING
