type: short
hook: Our first snow together
thumbnail text: First Snow
mood voices: on
place: snowy hill
time: day
music: calm

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
shot: medium on both
yuki (excited): Ren, look! It snowed last night! Our first snow together!
ren (excited): It's beautiful!
ren and yuki hop
pop: BRRR! near yuki
yuki (shy): It's a little cold, though.
ren (thinking): I would give you my scarf, but it's very important to my look.
yuki (pouty): Ren.
ren (shy): Okay, okay. I have a better idea.
ren holds out its paw to yuki
ren (shy): Hold my paw. Paws are warmer together.
yuki (happy): Much warmer.
ren and yuki walk to right paw in paw
hearts float up
ending: Tag your winter cuddle buddy!
