title: The Wish
type: short
hook: When you ask what they wished for
thumbnail text: What Did You Wish For?
mood voices: on
place: river
time: night
music: magical

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
ren holds the lantern
shot: medium on both
the lantern glows
yuki (happy): They say if you make a wish by lantern light, it comes true.
ren (excited): Then let's make one! Close your eyes.
ren covers its eyes
+ yuki covers its eyes
beat
ren (playful): What did you wish for?
yuki (shy): I can't tell you. Then it won't come true.
ren (pouty): Not even a little hint?
music: heartfelt
yuki walks to ren
yuki (shy): Okay. A tiny hint. It already came true.
ren (surprised): Already? How?
yuki (shy): I'm looking at it.
hearts float up
ending: Tag your wish come true!
