#  Ren & Yuki Short - The Flower Says   (vertical)
#  Ren asks a flower whether Yuki thinks he's brave (uses the flower).

title: The Flower Says
type: short
hook: The flower knows the answer...
thumbnail text: The Flower Says...
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: sunset
music: calm

ren enters from left to left
ren holds the flower
ren (whispers): Okay, flower. Tell me the truth.
ren (whispers): Yuki thinks I'm brave.
ren (whispers, worried): Yuki thinks I'm not brave.
ren (whispers): Brave.
ren (whispers, worried): Not brave.
beat
shot: close on ren
ren (surprised): Wait. That was the last petal.
ren (sad): The flower says not brave.
yuki enters from right to right
yuki (playful): What does the flower say?
ren jumps in fright
camera: shake
ren (shouts, scared): Nothing! The flower is very quiet! It's a quiet flower!
shot: medium on both
yuki walks to ren
yuki (thinking): Ren, you walked into the dark forest with me. Twice.
ren (shy): It was a very small forest.
yuki (happy): You're the bravest fox I know.
ren feels happy
hearts float up
ren (proud): See, flower? I told you.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (playful): Also the only fox I know.
