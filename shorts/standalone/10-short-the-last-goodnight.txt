title: The Last Goodnight
type: short
hook: Who says the LAST goodnight?
thumbnail text: Goodnight!
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: night
music: bedtime

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
ren and yuki sit down
yuki (sleepy): Goodnight, Ren.
ren (sleepy): Goodnight, Yuki.
beat
yuki (playful): Goodnight.
ren (playful): Goodnight.
yuki (playful): Goodnight!
shot: close on ren
ren (determined): Goodnight. Final goodnight. The last one.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (whispers, playful): Goodnight.
shot: medium on both
ren feels pouty
ren (pouty): Yuki! I said it was the last one!
yuki (laughing): Hehe!
ren lies down
ren (sleepy): Fine. You win. I'm going to sleep now.
beat
ren (whispers, playful): ...Goodnight.
yuki (shouts, surprised): Ren!
ren (laughing): Hahaha!
shot: close on yuki
yuki (whispers, playful): Goodnight.
