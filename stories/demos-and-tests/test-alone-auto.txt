title: Alone With Auto Editing
place: meadow
time: day
editing: auto

ren enters from left to center
ren (happy): Good morning, world! Ren is here, and today is going to be legendary!
ren (surprised): Wait. Did that cloud just wink at me? Clouds are not supposed to wink at anybody.
ren (shy): Okay, nobody saw that. Nobody saw me talking to a cloud in the middle of the meadow.
ren (sad): It is a little lonely, being the greatest explorer in the whole meadow with nobody to tell.
yuki enters from right to right
yuki (surprised): Oh! I didn't know anybody else was here!
ren (shy): Hello. I was just practising my explorer speech.
yuki (happy): Can I hear the rest of it?
narrator: And that was how the speech got its very first audience.
