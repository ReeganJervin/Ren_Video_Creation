title: The Speech
type: short
hook: Ren thanks his biggest fan...
thumbnail text: Thank You, Fans!
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: sunset
music: calm

shot: close on ren
ren (proud): Thank you. Thank you, everyone. Please, sit down.
ren (shy): First, I'd like to thank... my very fluffy tail.
yuki enters from right to right
+ ren (thinking): Who else? Who else...
yuki (playful): What about your exploring partner?
ren jumps in fright
camera: shake
ren (shouts, surprised): Yuki! How long have you been there?
shot: medium on both
yuki (playful): Since "my very fluffy tail".
ren (shy): You were next. Definitely next.
yuki (thinking): After your tail?
ren (shy): It's a very important tail.
yuki (laughing): Hehe!
ren turns to you
ren (proud): I'd like to thank Yuki. The best exploring partner in the world.
hearts float up
shot: close on yuki
yuki (shy): ...And the tail?
shot: close on ren
ren (whispers, proud): The tail is a close second.
