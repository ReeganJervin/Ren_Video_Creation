# The Ren & Yuki Instagram account

Everything to set up the account and start posting. The pictures are in `instagram\profile-kit\`. To make them again, double-click `make-profile.bat`. To change the moments they show, edit `instagram\profile-moments.txt`.

## 1. The account

1. Make a new Instagram account, then switch it to a **professional account**: Settings, then **Account type and tools**, then **Switch to professional account**, then **Creator**.
2. **Category:** *Digital creator* (or *Video creator*). You can hide the label on the profile if you like.
3. **Username** (pick the first one that is free; use the same name as your YouTube channel if you can):
   - `renandyuki`
   - `ren.and.yuki`
   - `renandyuki.official`
   - `renandyuki.couplethings`
4. **Name** (the bold line at the top; people can find you by the words in it):
   **Ren & Yuki | Cute Couple Animation**

## 2. Profile picture

Use **the same picture as your YouTube channel**, `channel\profile-picture-800.png`, so people recognise you on both. A second choice, made for Instagram's circle: `profile-picture.jpg` in this folder (Ren and Yuki together, smiling).

## 3. Bio (pick one; all are under Instagram's 150 characters)

**Option A: who they are** (recommended)
```
🦊 Ren: dramatic, always hungry
🐰 Yuki: sweet, always right
💛 Cute couple moments every week
▶️ Full stories on YouTube ⬇️
```

**Option B: start with Part 1**
```
A fox 🦊 + a bunny 🐰 = Couple Things 💛
Relatable couple moments, every week
Start with Part 1: How We Met 📌
▶️ More on YouTube ⬇️
```

**Option C: short and shareable**

```
Tag your Ren. Tag your Yuki. 🦊🐰
Cute couple animation 💛

▶️ YouTube ⬇️
```

## 4. Link in bio

Edit profile, then **Links**, then **Add external link**:
1. **Your YouTube channel** (title: "Full stories on YouTube")
2. Later: **the "Ren & Yuki: Couple Things" playlist** (title: "All Couple Things")

## 5. Highlights (the circles under the bio)

Highlights are made from Stories. For each one, share at least one Story first (for example, share the Reel to your Story). Then, on your profile, tap **New** (the + circle), choose the Stories, tap **Edit cover**, and pick the picture:

| Highlight | Cover | What goes in it |
|---|---|---|
| Our Story | `highlight-our-story.jpg` | Parts 1, 2 and 3 (how they met) |
| Couple Things | `highlight-couple-things.jpg` | Every Couple Things Reel |
| Ren | `highlight-ren.jpg` | Ren's funniest moments, polls about Ren |
| Yuki | `highlight-yuki.jpg` | Yuki's best moments, polls about Yuki |
| Episodes | `highlight-episodes.jpg` | "New episode on YouTube" Stories |

## 6. The first posts: the story starts with them meeting

The account opens with how Ren and Yuki met, so every new follower can start from the beginning:

| Order | Post | Files |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Reel: Part 1, How We Met** | `shorts\couple-things\1-couple-how-we-met.txt` |
| 2 | **Carousel: Meet Ren & Yuki** | `meet-us-1.jpg` to `meet-us-4.jpg` (in this folder) |
| 3 | **Reel: Part 2, Our First Date** | `2-couple-our-first-date.txt` |
| 4 | **Reel: Part 3, I Like Your Ears** | `3-couple-i-like-your-ears.txt` |
| 5 | Reel: Who Said It First? (continues the joke from Part 3) | `4-couple-who-said-it-first.txt` |
| 6 on | The other Couple Things (5, 6, 7…, in file-number order), with your other Shorts in between | see "Posting plan" in `STORY-IDEAS.md` |

For each Reel: make the video with `make-video.bat` and the Instagram files with `make-social.bat`. The caption, cover and carousel are in `videos\<name>\instagram\`, next to the video.

**Caption for the "Meet Ren & Yuki" carousel:**
```
Meet Ren & Yuki 🦊🐰
A dramatic fox and a bunny who is always right.
Their story starts with Part 1: How We Met 📌

Who are you more like? Tell us below 👇

#RenAndYuki #CoupleThings #CuteCouple #CoupleGoals #Animation
```

**Pin these 3** (tap ⋯ on the post, then **Pin to your profile**), so they always stay at the top:
1. Part 1: How We Met
2. Meet Ren & Yuki (the carousel)
3. Your most-watched Couple Things Reel (change it as new ones do better)

## 7. Stories (between posts, to keep people coming back)

Stories last 24 hours. A few quick ones each week keep the account active:
- **Poll:** "Who's right? 🦊 Ren / 🐰 Yuki" with a still from the latest Reel
- **Quiz:** "What will Ren forget next?"
- **Question box:** "Send us your couple moment, maybe Ren & Yuki will act it out!" (great story ideas)
- **Countdown:** "Part 2 tomorrow!"
- **Share each new Reel to your Story**, then add it to its Highlight.

## 8. Every post

- The same 5 hashtags (Instagram uses at most 5): `#RenAndYuki #CoupleThings #CuteCouple #CoupleGoals #Relatable` (the caption files already have them)
- Answer comments in the first hour.
- The voices are made by a computer: check Instagram's **"AI info"** label before posting.
