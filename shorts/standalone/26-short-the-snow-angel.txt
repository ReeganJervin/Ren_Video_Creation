title: The Snow Angel
type: short
hook: The best snow angel EVER
thumbnail text: Snow Angel!
mood voices: on
place: snowy hill
time: day
music: happy

shot: close on ren
ren (proud): Watch this. The most beautiful snow angel in history.
ren lies down
ren (happy): Wings... wings... wings...
shot: medium on both
yuki (thinking): Ren, you're just lying there.
ren (proud): The wings are in my mind.
beat
ren (worried): Yuki. I can't feel my tail.
yuki (playful): That's because you're lying in the snow.
ren (sad): Then I live here now.
yuki lies down
yuki (happy): Then I live here too.
ren (happy): Two snow angels.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (playful): One snow angel. And one snow potato.
