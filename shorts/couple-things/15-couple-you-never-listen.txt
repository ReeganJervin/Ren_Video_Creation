title: You Never Listen
type: short
hook: When she says "you never listen"
thumbnail text: "You Never Listen!"
mood voices: on
place: river
time: sunset
music: happy

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
shot: medium on both
yuki (pouty): Ren, you never listen to me.
ren (proud): Never listen? Let me see.
ren counts on its paws
ren (proud): This morning you said the clouds look like cotton candy.
ren (proud): At lunch you said your left ear was itchy. The left one.
ren (playful): And at four o'clock you said you wanted a cookie.
yuki (surprised): You remember all of that?
ren holds the cookie
music: heartfelt
ren gives the cookie to yuki
ren (shy): I always listen. I just don't always answer.
yuki (shy): ...That's actually really sweet.
beat
ren (playful): You also said I'm the most handsome fox in the meadow.
impact on yuki
yuki (laughing): I never said that!
ending: Tag the one who never listens (or always does)!
