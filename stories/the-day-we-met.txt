narrator: He trotted through the grass, looking at everything as though he had never seen it before.
ren (surprised): Oh! Look at this grass!
ren (playful): Green. Very green. Excellent grass.
ren (happy): Five stars.
ren looks around
ren (surprised): Ah-ha! A mysterious flower!
ren (playful): Petals... stem... very suspicious.
ren (shy): I should probably investigate further.
wait 1
ren (surprised): Hm. It appears to be a very quiet flower.
ren (playful): Perhaps it's shy. I understand. I can be shy too.
ren (happy): Not usually, though. I'm normally extremely impressive.
narrator: Ren continued exploring, finding something interesting in every corner of the meadow.
ren walks to center
ren (happy): Wow! From up here, I can see almost everything!
ren (playful): Meadow!

ren (happy): And the meadow I just came from!

ren looks around
ren (happy): But the forest is over there. And the river is somewhere beyond those trees.
ren (surprised): And who knows what lies past the hills?
ren (happy): One day, I'll go all the way out there. I'll see amazing things. I'll discover places nobody knows about!
ren (happy): And then I'll tell everybody about it!
ren (sad): Everybody...

ren (happy): Never mind! There's plenty more exploring to do!

# --- Scene 3: A very important discovery ---

narrator: By the time the sun had climbed higher, Ren had explored nearly every corner of the meadow he could reach.
ren looks around
ren (surprised): I've found something!
ren (happy): A secret discovery!
ren (playful): What could it be? A rare treasure? A mysterious clue? Something nobody has ever seen before?
ren (surprised): It's a leaf.
ren (sad): A very ordinary leaf.
ren (shy): But wait... what if it's not an ordinary leaf?
ren (surprised): What if it's a rare leaf? A special leaf? A leaf that has been here longer than any other leaf?
ren (happy): The oldest leaf in the meadow!
ren (happy): This is incredible!
ren looks around
ren (happy): Did you see that? Did you see what I found?
wait 1
ren (sad): Oh. Right.
ren (shy): Well... I saw it.
ren (happy): And that's what matters, isn't it?
ren (playful): There you go, little leaf. Stay mysterious.
narrator: Ren saw another flower. He spotted another interesting patch of grass. He found another place that would have been exciting to show somebody.
ren (shy): Look at this!
wait 1
ren (sad): Nobody's here.
narrator: Every time he found something interesting, he caught himself wanting to turn around and share it.
ren (sad): I wish somebody could see these things with me.
narrator: Ren had spent the entire morning talking to the world. For the first time, he wished the world would talk back.

# --- Scene 4: The quiet nobody talks about ---

ren walks to center
ren feels sad
narrator: The afternoon passed slowly. The meadow was still beautiful, but Ren began to notice how quiet it was.
ren (sad): It's funny.
ren (shy): I always thought being an explorer would mean having adventures every day.
ren (happy): And I do have adventures.
ren (happy): I find interesting things. I go to interesting places. I make up stories about what might be waiting for me.
ren (sad): But sometimes...
ren (sad): Sometimes I wish there was somebody here.
ren (shy): Somebody I could show things to.
ren looks around
ren (shy): Somebody who would laugh at my silly discoveries. Or tell me that my ideas were silly.
ren (playful): Maybe somebody who would come exploring with me.
ren (sad): But there's nobody.
narrator: Ren had never really thought about it before. He was so busy looking for new places that he had forgotten how nice it would be to have someone beside him.
ren (shy): I wonder if there's anybody else out there who feels like this.
ren (sad): Somewhere in the forests, beside the rivers, or beyond the hills, there might be another little animal exploring the world alone.
ren (shy): Someone who also has stories to tell.
ren (sad): Someone who also wants a friend.
ren (happy): Well... tomorrow is another day.
ren (shy): Maybe tomorrow will be different.
narrator: Ren didn't know that, before the day was over, his world really would change.

# --- Scene 5: A little bunny in the meadow ---

time: sunset
yuki enters from right to right
narrator: The sun began to soften, painting the meadow in warm golden light. Ren was wandering through the grass when he noticed someone nearby.
ren looks at yuki
yuki looks at ren
ren (surprised): Oh! Hello.
yuki (surprised): Hello.
ren (shy): Um... I haven't seen you here before.
yuki (shy): I haven't seen you either.
ren (shy): Nice weather.
yuki (happy): Yes. It is.
ren (playful): Very... sunny.
wait 1
ren (shy): Sorry. I'm not usually this bad at talking.
yuki (surprised): Really?
ren (playful): Well, I talk all the time! Sometimes I talk so much that I have to stop talking just to hear myself think.
ren (shy): That probably doesn't make any sense.
yuki (happy): It's all right.
ren (shy): I'm Ren, by the way.
yuki (shy): I'm Yuki.
ren (happy): Yuki. That's a nice name.
yuki (happy): Ren is a nice name too.
ren (happy): Thank you.
narrator: For the first time, the silence didn't feel quite so uncomfortable.
ren (shy): Do you live around here?
yuki (shy): Not too far away.
ren (happy): Oh! Do you come here often?
yuki (shy): Sometimes.
ren (shy): What do you do here?
yuki (shy): I look around. I explore a little.
ren (surprised): You explore?
yuki (happy): Yes.
ren (happy): That's nice. I explore too.
yuki (shy): On your own?
ren (sad): Yes. Mostly.
yuki (sad): Oh. Me too.
narrator: Neither of them knew what to say next. But those two little words had brought them closer than they realised.

# --- Scene 6: Two little explorers ---

ren (shy): What do you like exploring?
yuki (shy): I like looking around and finding interesting things.
ren (happy): Me too! I like the meadow, the forest, the river, and any place that looks interesting.
ren (playful): Sometimes I even explore places that don't look interesting at first.
yuki (surprised): Why?
ren (happy): Because they might become interesting!
yuki (happy): That makes sense.
ren (happy): Exactly! You never know what you'll find.
ren (playful): I found this amazing leaf today. Well, it was a normal leaf, but I thought it might be the oldest leaf in the entire meadow.
yuki (surprised): The oldest leaf?
ren (happy): Possibly.
yuki (playful): Did you find out how old it was?
ren (shy): No.
yuki (playful): Then how do you know it was the oldest?
ren (surprised): Hm. That's an excellent question.
yuki (happy): Hehe!
ren (surprised): Are you laughing at my discovery?
yuki (playful): A little.
ren (playful): Well, I never! That was a very serious investigation.
yuki (happy): Hehe!
ren (happy): Oh, all right. Maybe it wasn't the greatest discovery.

ren (happy): I like making people laugh.
yuki (shy): I like laughing.
ren (happy): That's a good combination!
yuki (happy): I think so too.
ren (shy): Do you ever find something interesting and wish you could show it to someone?
yuki (sad): Yes. All the time.
ren (surprised): Really?
yuki (sad): Sometimes I see a beautiful place or something funny. I want to tell someone about it, but there's nobody around.
ren (sad): I know exactly how that feels.
yuki (shy): You do?
ren (sad): Sometimes I find something exciting, and I turn around to show someone... and there's nobody there.
yuki (sad): That happens to me too.
narrator: For the first time, neither of them felt that they had to pretend to be happier than they were.
narrator: Ren wasn't the only little explorer who spent his days alone. And Yuki wasn't the only one who wished she had a friend.
ren (happy): Maybe we just hadn't found each other yet.
yuki (happy): Maybe.
