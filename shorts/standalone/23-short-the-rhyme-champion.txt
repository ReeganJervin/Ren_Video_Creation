# ============================================================
#  Ren & Yuki Short - The Rhyme Champion   (~30 s, vertical)
#  Ren can rhyme any word. Any word at all.
#  In The Compliment's and Very Local Wind's style: the first line is the
#  joke (no walking in), one joke that grows, a last line that loops.
#
#  Commands (run them in the project folder, or drag the story onto the .bat file):
#    Check it:            check-story.bat shorts\standalone\23-short-the-rhyme-champion.txt
#    Watch it:            preview.bat shorts\standalone\23-short-the-rhyme-champion.txt
#    Make the video:      make-video.bat shorts\standalone\23-short-the-rhyme-champion.txt
#    If it stopped:       resume-video.bat shorts\standalone\23-short-the-rhyme-champion.txt
#    YouTube files only:  node tools\make-video.js shorts\standalone\23-short-the-rhyme-champion.txt --youtube
#    Instagram files:     make-social.bat shorts\standalone\23-short-the-rhyme-champion.txt

title: The Rhyme Champion
type: short
hook: He can rhyme ANY word...
thumbnail text: Rhyme Champion
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: sunset
music: happy

shot: close on ren
ren (proud): I can rhyme any word. Any word at all.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (playful): Cat.
shot: close on ren
ren (proud): Hat. Too easy.
yuki (playful): Moon.
ren (proud): Spoon. Next.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (thinking): Orange.
beat
shot: close on ren
ren (thinking): ...Door hinge.
shot: medium on both
yuki (laughing): That's not a rhyme!
ren (pouty): It's a very advanced rhyme.
yuki (playful): Purple.
beat
ren (shy): I can rhyme any word. Except purple.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (playful): And orange.
