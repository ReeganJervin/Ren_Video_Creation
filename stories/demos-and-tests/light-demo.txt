title: Little Lights
place: forest
time: night
light: dim
music: mystery
mood voices: on

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from left to right
+ narrator: The night was so dark that even the fireflies stayed close to home.
ren holds the lantern
light: dark
yuki (scared): Ren? Ren, where did the forest go?
ren (worried): It's still here. It's just... hiding.
ren lights the lantern
ren (proud): There. A little light, for a little fox.
yuki (whispers): And a little bunny.
ren walks to yuki
yuki (happy): Much better.

orb appears at center
the orb glows
music: magical
yuki (surprised): Look! Something is shining.
yuki walks to the orb
yuki looks at the orb
yuki (thinking): It's warm. Like it's been waiting for us.
ren puts out the lantern
ren (playful): See? We don't even need my lantern now.
the orb stops glowing
yuki (scared): Ren! Light it again! Light it again!
ren lights the lantern
ren (laughing): Hehe! Sorry, sorry.

sword appears next to ren
ren puts down the lantern
ren picks up the sword
ren holds up the sword
music: epic
ren (determined): Starfang! Show us the way!
yuki (surprised): It only glows for you.
ren (shy): I know. I don't know why either.
the orb glows
yuki (happy): Three little lights. That's enough for anything.
light: dim
narrator: And with three little lights, the dark didn't feel so dark any more.
