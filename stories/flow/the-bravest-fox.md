# The Bravest Fox: a Ren & Yuki story for Google Flow

A Short of about 48 seconds: **6 clips of 8 seconds**. It follows the style of the Shorts that did best ("Very Local Wind", "The Compliment"): a question as the hook, Ren pretending to be brave, excuses that get sillier each time, and one short, funny last line.

**Hook text (for the title or the first second):** Ren is NOT scared. Probably.

**Format:** vertical 9:16 if Flow offers it (for Shorts), otherwise 16:9.

**Reference pictures:** add `reference/ren-sheet.png` and `reference/yuki-sheet.png` from this project as character references ("ingredients"), so Ren and Yuki look the same in every clip.

---

## Paste these three blocks at the start of every clip's prompt

**Style:**
> Cute 2D chibi storybook cartoon, soft round shapes, one warm dark-brown outline on everything (never black), flat warm pastel colours with a gentle top-to-bottom gradient, small soft white highlights, pink blush on the cheeks. Cozy and gentle, never scary, never realistic. Smooth, simple animation.

**Ren:**
> Ren, a small chibi fox (boy): big round head, bright orange fur, cream-white cheeks, muzzle and tummy, tall pointed ears with dark brown tips and pink insides, big shiny dark-brown eyes, pink blush, dark brown paws and feet, a big fluffy orange tail with a white tip, and a burgundy scarf tied round his neck with a small cream flower on it. Proud and a little dramatic. Voice: young, bright, a bit cheeky.

**Yuki:**
> Yuki, a small chibi bunny (girl): round soft white fur with a faint pink shade, long upright ears with pink insides, a pink cherry-blossom flower on her right ear, big shiny dark-brown eyes, pink blush, a pink bow at her neck with a little flower, a round cotton tail. She hops instead of walking. Calm, clever and teasing. Voice: young, soft and sweet, a little playful.

**Place (same in every clip):**
> A cozy forest path at dusk: tall rounded trees, a sandy path, ferns and mushrooms, a soft purple-pink sky glowing between the leaves, a few fireflies floating. Peaceful and pretty, not dark or spooky.

---

## Clip 1: The snap (8 s)
> Ren and Yuki walk side by side along the forest path toward the camera, Yuki hopping. A twig snaps somewhere off screen (a small "crack" sound). Ren freezes mid-step, his ears shoot straight up and his eyes go wide. Yuki stops and looks at him.
>
> Yuki (soft, curious): "Ren... did you hear that?"
> Ren (too quickly, voice a little high): "Hear what? I heard nothing. Very quiet forest."
>
> Medium shot of both, gentle camera.

## Clip 2: The tail (8 s)
> A bush beside the path rustles. Ren's fluffy tail suddenly puffs up to twice its size, like a giant orange cotton ball. Yuki looks at the huge tail, then slowly up at Ren, unimpressed.
>
> Yuki (deadpan): "Your tail just got twice as big."
> Ren (nose in the air, proud): "It's fluffing. For warmth."
>
> Medium shot; the tail puff is the funny moment, give it a little bounce.

## Clip 3: Behind Yuki (8 s)
> Ren tiptoes behind Yuki and peeks out over her shoulder, holding on to her ears like a shield. Yuki turns her head to look at him with one eyebrow raised.
>
> Yuki (playful): "Why are you behind me?"
> Ren (very serious whisper): "Strategy. The bravest one goes at the back."
>
> Close shot on both faces.

## Clip 4: Two little eyes (8 s)
> Mysterious and cute, not scary. The bush rustles again and two tiny round shining eyes blink inside the leaves. Ren covers his whole face with his big fluffy tail and trembles a little. Yuki leans slowly toward the bush, curious and calm.
>
> Ren (muffled, from behind his tail): "Tell the forest I was brave."
>
> Soft gentle suspense music, slow push in toward the bush.

## Clip 5: The surprise (8 s)
> A tiny round baby hedgehog pops out of the bush, blinks, and does a tiny cute sneeze ("achoo!"). Yuki smiles warmly. Ren slowly lowers his tail and peeks out with one eye.
>
> Yuki (gentle, laughing a little): "It's a baby hedgehog, Ren."
>
> Close shot on the hedgehog, then on Ren's one peeking eye.

## Clip 6: The last word (8 s)
> Ren steps out in front, puffs out his chest and stands tall like a hero, paws on his hips, scarf blowing a little. Yuki gives him a long, flat, unimpressed look. The baby hedgehog sneezes again ("achoo!") and Ren jumps straight up in fright, then lands pretending nothing happened.
>
> Ren (proud, heroic): "I knew that. I was protecting it... from you."
>
> End on Yuki's flat look and Ren's sheepish smile. (The sneeze-and-jump leads nicely back into Clip 1 if the Short loops.)

---

## Tips for Flow
- Keep each clip's dialogue to these one or two short lines. 8 seconds is short, and long lines get cut.
- If a clip gets Ren or Yuki wrong (black outlines, a realistic fox, no scarf, no flower), make it again with the reference pictures added. Ren's **burgundy scarf** and Yuki's **cherry-blossom flower** are the easiest ways to recognise them.
- Use "extend" or "scene builder" to join the clips, and keep the same style and place blocks in every prompt so the forest and the light stay the same.
- Family show rules: no fighting, nothing truly scary. The hedgehog is cute, and the suspense in Clip 4 is gentle.
