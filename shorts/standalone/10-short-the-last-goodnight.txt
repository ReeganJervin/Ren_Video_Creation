#  Who says the last goodnight? It loops back to the start.
#  Rewritten 2026-10-30 in The Compliment's style: the first line is
#  the joke (no walking in), one joke that grows, and the last line

title: The Last Goodnight
type: short
hook: Who says the LAST goodnight?
thumbnail text: Goodnight!
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: night
music: bedtime

shot: medium on both
yuki (sleepy): Goodnight, Ren.
+ ren and yuki sit down
ren (sleepy): Goodnight, Yuki.
yuki (playful): Goodnight.
ren (playful): Goodnight.
shot: close on ren
ren (determined): Goodnight. Final goodnight. The very last one.
beat
shot: close on yuki
yuki (whispers, playful): Goodnight.
shot: medium on both
ren (pouty): Yuki! That was the last one!
ren lies down
ren (sleepy): Fine. I'm asleep now. Very asleep.
beat
ren (whispers, playful): ...Goodnight.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (shouts, surprised): Ren!
beat
yuki (whispers, playful): Goodnight, Ren.
