title: The Perfect Hiding Spot
type: short
hook: Ren found the PERFECT hiding spot
thumbnail text: Perfect Hiding Spot
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: day
music: happy

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
yuki covers her eyes
yuki (happy): Hide and seek! One... two... three...
ren (excited): The perfect hiding spot. Nobody will ever find me here.
ren sneaks to yuki
ren hides behind yuki
yuki (happy): ...nine... ten! Ready or not!
yuki looks around
yuki (thinking): Hmm. Where could he be?
shot: close on yuki
yuki (playful): Is he behind the tree? No.
yuki (playful): Is he behind the flowers? No.
yuki looks right
beat
shot: medium on both
yuki (playful): Ren, I can see your tail.
ren peeks out
ren (whispers, proud): No you can't. I'm a bush.
yuki (laughing): Bushes don't talk!
ren (whispers, shy): ...Very talkative bush.
yuki turns to ren
yuki (playful): Found you!
ren (pouty): Best two out of three?
