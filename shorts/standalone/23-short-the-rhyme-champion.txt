title: The Rhyme Champion
type: short
hook: He can rhyme ANY word...
thumbnail text: Rhyme Champion
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: sunset
music: happy

shot: close on ren
ren (proud): I can rhyme any word. Any word at all.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (playful): Cat.
shot: close on ren
ren (proud): Hat. Too easy.
yuki (playful): Moon.
ren (proud): Spoon. Next.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (thinking): Orange.
beat
shot: close on ren
ren (thinking): ...Door hinge.
shot: medium on both
yuki (laughing): That's not a rhyme!
ren (pouty): It's a very advanced rhyme.
yuki (playful): Purple.
beat
ren (shy): I can rhyme any word. Except purple.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (playful): And orange.
