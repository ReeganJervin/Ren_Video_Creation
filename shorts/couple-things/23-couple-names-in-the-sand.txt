title: Names in the Sand
type: short
hook: When he writes your names in the sand
thumbnail text: Names in the Sand
mood voices: on
place: beach
time: sunset
music: calm

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
shot: medium on both
ren (excited): Yuki, look! I'm writing our names in the sand.
ren crouches
ren writes in the air
ren (proud): Ren and Yuki. With a heart in the middle.
yuki (shy): That's so sweet.
narrator: Then a wave came, and washed the names away.
ren stands up
ren (sad): Oh. It's gone.
yuki (sad): The sea took it.
music: heartfelt
ren walks to yuki
ren (determined): Then I'll write it again. Every time the sea takes it, I'll write it again.
yuki (shy): Even if it takes forever?
ren (happy): Especially then.
hearts float up
ending: Tag the one who'd write your name again!
