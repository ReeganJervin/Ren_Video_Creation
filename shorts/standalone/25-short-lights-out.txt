# ============================================================
#  Ren & Yuki Short - Lights Out   (~30 s, vertical)
#  In the cave, with his lantern, nothing scares Ren. Then he blows it out.
#  In The Compliment's and Very Local Wind's style: the first line is the
#  joke (no walking in), one joke that grows, a last line that loops.
#
#  Commands (run them in the project folder, or drag the story onto the .bat file):
#    Check it:            check-story.bat shorts\standalone\25-short-lights-out.txt
#    Watch it:            preview.bat shorts\standalone\25-short-lights-out.txt
#    Make the video:      make-video.bat shorts\standalone\25-short-lights-out.txt
#    If it stopped:       resume-video.bat shorts\standalone\25-short-lights-out.txt
#    YouTube files only:  node tools\make-video.js shorts\standalone\25-short-lights-out.txt --youtube
#    Instagram files:     make-social.bat shorts\standalone\25-short-lights-out.txt
# ============================================================

title: Lights Out
type: short
hook: Nothing in this cave is scary...
thumbnail text: Lights OUT!
mood voices: on
place: cave tunnel
music: mystery

ren holds the lantern
+ the lantern glows
+ ren (proud): With my lantern, nothing in this cave is scary.
yuki (thinking): What about that drippy noise?
ren (proud): Not scary.
yuki (thinking): And the dark?
shot: close on ren
ren (proud): Pfff. I'm not even scared of the dark. Watch.
ren blows out the lantern
beat
ren (whispers, scared): ...Yuki?
yuki (whispers, playful): Yes?
ren (shouts, scared): Where did the cave go?!
yuki (laughing): Light it again, brave fox.
ren lights the lantern
shot: medium on both
ren (proud): See? Not scary at all.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (playful): Your ears are shaking.
shot: close on ren
ren (shy): Very local cave wind.
