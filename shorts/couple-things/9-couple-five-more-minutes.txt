hook: POV: "Just five more minutes"
thumbnail text: Five More Minutes...
mood voices: on
place: cottage garden
time: day
music: calm

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
shot: medium on both
ren sits down
ren feels sleepy
yuki (excited): Good morning! Get up, the picnic starts soon!
pop: ZZZ! near ren
ren (sleepy): Five more minutes.
yuki (pouty): Okay. Five minutes.
narrator: Five minutes later...
yuki (happy): Ren! It's time!
ren (sleepy): Five more minutes.
narrator: Five hours later...
time: sunset
yuki (pouty): Ren. The picnic is over.

shot: close on ren
impact on ren

ren stands up

yuki (laughing): Only if you wake up first.
