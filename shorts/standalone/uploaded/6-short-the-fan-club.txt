title: The Fan Club
type: short
hook: Ren's fan club has ONE member
thumbnail text: Ren's Fan Club
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: day
music: happy

shot: close on ren
ren (proud): Welcome to the first meeting of the Ren Fan Club!
ren raises his paws
ren (proud): Members, please stand up.
beat
ren (shy): ...Thank you, member.
yuki enters from right to right
yuki (playful): Can I join?
shot: medium on both
ren (proud): Sorry. It's very exclusive. You have to be a very big fan of Ren.
yuki (thinking): How many members are there?
shot: close on ren
ren (whispers, shy): ...One.
shot: medium on both
yuki (playful): Then I'll be the president.
ren (surprised): President? That's a very big promotion!
hearts float up
ren (shy): ...Best fan club in the meadow.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (playful): Meeting over. Members, please stand up.
