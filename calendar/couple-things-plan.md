# Couple Things plan: YouTube + Instagram, 11 November – 3 December 2026

India time. **The same Couple Things video goes on YouTube and Instagram at the same time, every evening.** The original
YouTube plan (episodes, episode Shorts, standalone Shorts) is **on hold** for these weeks (`UPLOAD-SCHEDULE.md`).
Already posted: CT1 How We Met and CT2 Our First Date. Story files: `shorts\couple-things\` (CT3 = `3-couple-...txt`).

## Every day

| Time | YouTube | Instagram |
|---|---|---|
| **7:00 PM** | **The day's Couple Things** as a Short, in the **"Ren & Yuki: Couple Things"** playlist | **The same video** as a Reel (cover `reel-cover.jpg`, caption `instagram.txt`) |
| **12:00 PM**, Thu–Sun | (nothing: these are on YouTube already) | **In between:** one of your best Shorts (Thu, Sat) or a carousel (Fri, Sun) |
| Evening | Answer comments | Share the Reel to your Story, add it to the "Couple Things" Highlight, one Story with a sticker; answer comments for the first hour |
| **9:00 PM** | **Upload night:** schedule tomorrow's Short for 7 PM | Schedule tomorrow's Reel (and 12 PM post) |

**For every YouTube Short:** title and description from `videos\<story>\<story>-description.txt`; choose the playlist on the
Details page; set **Related video** to *How We Met* (CT1), so new viewers can start from the beginning; schedule for 7:00 PM.
**For every Instagram Reel:** the same video, cover `reel-cover.jpg` (Edit cover → Add from camera roll), caption from
`instagram.txt` (both in `videos\<story>\instagram\`); schedule it with Advanced settings → Schedule.
**After posting:** move the story file into `shorts\couple-things\uploaded\`, like the others.

**Make the videos a few days ahead:** `make-video.bat` (the video and YouTube files), then `make-social.bat` (Instagram).
