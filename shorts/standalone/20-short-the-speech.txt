title: The Speech
type: short
hook: Ren practises his famous-explorer speech
thumbnail text: Thank You, Fans!
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: sunset
music: calm

ren enters from left to center
ren turns to you
ren (proud): Thank you. Thank you, everyone. Please, sit down.
ren (proud): When I became the most legendary explorer in the world, I never forgot the little meadow.
ren (shy): And I'd like to thank... my very fluffy tail.
beat
ren (thinking): Who else? Who else...
yuki enters from right to right
yuki (playful): What about your exploring partner?
ren jumps in fright
camera: shake
ren (shouts, surprised): Yuki! How long have you been there?
yuki (playful): Since "my very fluffy tail".
shot: close on ren
ren (shy): I was just getting to you. You were next.
yuki (thinking): After your tail?
ren (shy): It's a very important tail.
shot: medium on both
yuki (laughing): Hehe!
ren walks to yuki
ren (happy): Okay. Second try.
ren turns to you
ren (proud): I'd like to thank Yuki. The best exploring partner in the world.
hearts float up
shot: close on yuki
yuki (shy): ...And the tail?
shot: close on ren
ren (whispers, proud): The tail is a close second.
