title: The Missing Cookie
editing: auto

scene: A Cookie Mystery
place: meadow
time: day
music: happy

ren enters
+ narrator: One sunny afternoon, Ren was very excited about something.
yuki enters from right
yuki (happy): Hi, Ren! Why are you smiling so much?
ren (happy): I baked a cookie! The biggest cookie in the whole meadow!
yuki (surprised): The biggest cookie in the whole meadow?
ren (playful): Yes! I left it right here on this rock.
ren looks down
beat
ren looks around
beat
ren (surprised): Wait... where did it go?
shot: extreme close on yuki
yuki (shy): Um... I don't know anything about a cookie.
ren (pouty): Yuki! Do you have crumbs on your face?
yuki (shy): Maybe a tiny bit.
beat
ren (playful): Ha! Then let's bake another one together.

scene: The Wishing Star
music: magical
time: dusk

narrator: As the sky turned purple, something sparkled above the trees.
star appears
reveal: the star
yuki (surprised): Look, Ren! A little star!
star twinkles
ren (happy): Let's make a wish!
slow motion 4
star falls
narrator: The little star floated gently down, right into the soft grass.
ren picks up the star
ren (surprised): It's warm, like a little cookie!
yuki (sad): Oh no, is it lost?

scene: By the River
reveal: river
narrator: So the two friends carried it to the sparkling river.
ren (happy): Come on, little star. Your friends are waiting.
ren lets go of the star
narrator: The star rose up into the sky, and the friends waved goodbye.
ren and yuki wave
