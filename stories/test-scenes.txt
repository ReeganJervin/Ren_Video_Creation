scene: A Sunny Morning
place: meadow
time: day
music: happy

+ narrator: One sunny morning, Ren went out to play in the meadow.

+ yuki (happy): Good morning, Ren!
ren waves at yuki

yuki (playful): Yes! Let's go to the big forest!

scene: Into the Forest
place: forest
time: dusk
music: mystery

narrator: The forest was quiet, and the shadows were growing long.
yuki looks around

yuki (happy): Thank you, Ren. I feel braver now.
ren (playful): You're the bravest bunny I know!

narrator: Then, high above the trees, something began to shine.

time: night
music: magical

star appears

yuki (surprised): Look, Ren! A little star!
ren (happy): It's so beautiful!
star glows
narrator: The star sparkled, as if it was saying hello.
ren and yuki wave
yuki hugs ren
+ narrator: And the two friends watched it shine all night long.
