title: The Fortune Orb
type: short
hook: The orb sees Ren's future...
thumbnail text: Your Future...
mood voices: on
place: cottage garden
time: dusk
music: magical

# Yuki holds the orb like a fortune teller
yuki holds the orb
+ yuki (whispers): Ren... I can see your future.
the orb glows
shot: close on ren
ren (excited): Is it legendary? Tell me it's legendary.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (whispers): I see a fox...
ren (excited): Yes!
yuki (whispers): ...a very brave fox...
ren (proud): Obviously.
yuki (whispers, playful): ...who forgot the snacks.
beat
shot: medium on both
ren (pouty): That's not the future. That's today.
yuki (playful): The orb is never wrong.
ren (thinking): Ask it who's the bravest fox in the meadow.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (whispers, playful): It says... second bravest.
