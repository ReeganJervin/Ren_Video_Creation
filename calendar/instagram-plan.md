# Instagram plan: 9 – 29 November 2026

Instagram only (India time). YouTube has its own plan in `UPLOAD-SCHEDULE.md`. Account set-up, bio, Highlights and what
to pin: `instagram\INSTAGRAM-PROFILE.md`. Story files are in `shorts\couple-things\` (CT1, CT2… = the file numbers).

## Every day

| Time | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| **7:00 PM** | **Main Reel: the next Couple Things** (one a day, in number order) | Video `videos\<story>\<story>.mp4`, cover `reel-cover.jpg`, caption `instagram.txt` (both in `videos\<story>\instagram\`) |
| **12:00 PM**, Thu to Sun | **In between:** one of your best Shorts (Thu, Sat) or a carousel of a Couple Things (Fri, Sun) | Carousel: `carousel-01.jpg` to `carousel-08.jpg` from that story's `instagram\` folder |
| After each Reel | **Share it to your Story**, add it to its Highlight | Couple Things → "Couple Things" Highlight; Parts 1–3 → "Our Story" |
| Each evening | **One Story with a sticker** (from the list below) | A still from the day's Reel works well |
| First hour after a post | **Answer every comment** | Instagram shows a post to more people when people talk |

Schedule the next day's posts the night before (when sharing: **Advanced settings → Schedule**), or post at the time.
Before each week: run `make-video.bat` and `make-social.bat` on that week's Couple Things, and `make-social.bat` on the
in-between Shorts (their videos are made already).
