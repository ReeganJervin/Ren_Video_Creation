title: The Last Cookie
type: short
hook: One cookie. Two friends. Big problem.
thumbnail text: The LAST Cookie
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: day
music: tense

# a stand-off: they walk in from both sides while Ren speaks
cookie appears at center
ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
+ ren (determined): The last cookie.
shot: extreme close on yuki
yuki (determined): The last cookie.
shot: medium on both
ren (thinking): I found it, so I get the big half.
yuki (thinking): I saw it first, so I get the big half.
ren (pouty): Cookies don't have a big half!
ren and yuki look at each other
beat
owl flies in from right to right
owl: Hoo! Since you can't decide... I'll keep it safe for you.
the cookie disappears
owl flies away
camera: shake
narrator: Nobody saw that cookie ever again.
music: happy
ren (sad): We should have shared it.
yuki (sad): Next time, we share it straight away.
shot: close on ren
ren (playful): Deal. But I get the big half.
shot: close on yuki
yuki (laughing): Ren!
