hook: When he tries to give you a star
thumbnail text: A Star for You
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: night
music: magical

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
shot: medium on both
star appears
yuki (happy): Look, Ren. That star is so bright tonight.
ren (determined): Then I'll get it for you.
yuki (surprised): Ren, you can't catch a star.
ren stretches its paws wide
ren (determined): Watch me.
star falls
impact on ren
ren (surprised): It... it worked?
music: heartfelt
ren points at the star
ren (shy): For you.
yuki (shy): Ren... it belongs in the sky.
ren (thinking): You're right. Some things are too bright to keep.
star rises
yuki (shy): But you'll stay, right?
ren (happy): Every night.
hearts float up
ending: Tag the one who'd catch you a star!
