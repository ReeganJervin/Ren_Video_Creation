title: The Shiny Pebble
type: short
hook: Ren found a LEGENDARY treasure!
thumbnail text: Legendary Treasure!
mood voices: on

time: day
music: happy

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
ren finds the pebble
ren picks up the pebble
ren (excited): Yuki! I found a legendary treasure!
yuki walks to ren
yuki looks at the pebble
yuki (thinking): Ren. That's a pebble.
shot: close on ren
ren (proud): It's a legendary pebble.
yuki (playful): What makes it legendary?
ren (thinking): It's shiny. It's round. And I found it.
ren (proud): Those are the three rules of legendary pebbles.
shot: medium on both
yuki (laughing): Hehe!
beat
ren (shy): Here. It's for you.
ren gives the pebble to yuki
yuki (surprised): For me?
ren (shy): Every legendary explorer should have a treasure.
yuki feels shy
hearts float up
yuki (shy): I'll keep it forever.
shot: close on ren
ren (proud): Good. Because I'll need to borrow it on Tuesdays.
