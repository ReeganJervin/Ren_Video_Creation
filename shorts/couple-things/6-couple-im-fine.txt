title: I'm Fine
type: short
hook: When she says "I'm fine"...
thumbnail text: "I'm Fine."
mood voices: on
place: cottage garden
time: day
music: calm

yuki enters from right to right
yuki feels pouty
yuki turns away
ren enters from left to left
ren (happy): Hey! Is everything okay?
pop: HMPH! near yuki
yuki (pouty): I'm fine.
ren (thinks silently): That was not a fine "fine".
ren (worried): Are you sure?
yuki (pouty): I said I'm fine.
music: tense
ren counts on its paws
ren (thinks): Her birthday? No. Did I eat her snacks? No... Did I forget to call?
shot: close on ren
impact on ren
ren (thinks): I forgot to call.
shot: medium on both
music: heartfelt
ren walks to yuki
ren (shy): I'm sorry I didn't call last night.
yuki turns to ren
yuki (pouty): ...You remembered.
ren holds out its paw
ren (shy): I'll call every night. Even when I'm sleepy.
yuki (happy): Fine.
hearts float up
ren (whispers): Was that a good "fine"?
yuki (playful): That was a good "fine".
ending: Tag your "I'm fine" person!
