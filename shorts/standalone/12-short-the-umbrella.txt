title: The Umbrella
type: short
hook: Ren is ready for the storm!
thumbnail text: Storm Ready!
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: day
music: happy

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
ren holds the umbrella
ren opens the umbrella
ren (proud): Don't worry, Yuki. I'm ready for the storm.
yuki looks up
yuki (thinking): Ren, there isn't a single cloud in the sky.
ren (proud): That's what the storm wants you to think.
yuki (playful): It's sunny. Very sunny.
shot: close on ren
ren (thinking): Then it's a sun umbrella. For shade. And for style.
ren feels proud
shot: medium on both
yuki (laughing): Hehe! It does look very stylish.
yuki walks to ren
yuki (shy): Can I stand in the shade too?
ren (surprised): In my very stylish shade?
yuki (happy): Please?
ren (happy): Explorer's rule: the shade is better with two.
hearts float up
shot: close on yuki
yuki (playful): That's not a real rule.
shot: close on ren
ren (proud): It is now.
