title: Who Said It First?
type: short
hook: The debate every couple has
thumbnail text: I Said It First!
mood voices: on
place: river
time: sunset
music: happy

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
shot: medium on both
ren (proud): I said "I love you" first.
yuki (determined): No. I said it first.
ren (proud): Here, at the river. You were eating berries.
yuki (pouty): You said "I like your ears." That doesn't count.
ren (thinking): It was a very romantic ear compliment.
yuki (proud): I said it on the bridge. Clearly.
ren (playful): You said you love berries.
yuki (pouty): I was looking at you when I said it!
beat
ren walks to yuki
ren (shy): Okay. You win. I love you.
yuki (shy): I love you too.
hearts float up
beat
yuki (playful): ...But I said it first.
shot: close on ren
ren (thinks silently): I definitely said it first.
ending: Who said it first in your relationship?
