title: The Long Story
type: short
hook: When he tells you his LONGEST story
thumbnail text: The Longest Story
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: night
music: bedtime

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
shot: medium on both
ren and yuki sit down
ren (excited): Did I ever tell you how I fought the angry leaf?
yuki (happy): No. Tell me.
ren (proud): It was a dark and windy morning. Very windy. The windiest.
ren (determined): The leaf looked at me. I looked at the leaf.
yuki yawns
ren (proud): Then the leaf moved. Just a little. Very suspicious.
yuki feels sleepy
impact: small on ren
ren (excited): So I jumped to the left! Then to the right! Then left again!
yuki lies down
beat
pop: ZZZ! near yuki
ren (proud): And that is how I defeated the angry leaf. Yuki?
shot: close on ren
ren (whispers): ...Good night. Part two tomorrow.
shot: medium on both
yuki (sleepy): Please... no part two.
ending: Tag the one with the longest stories!
