title: The Snore
type: short
hook: When he snores but "doesn't snore"
thumbnail text: "I Don't Snore!"
mood voices: on
place: meadow
time: night
music: bedtime

ren enters from left to left
+ yuki enters from right to right
shot: medium on both
ren sits down
+ yuki sits down
ren (sleepy): Good night, Yuki.
yuki (sleepy): Good night, Ren.
ren feels sleepy
+ yuki feels sleepy
beat
pop: ZZZ! near ren
ren (thinks silently): Zzz... zzz... ZZZ...
yuki feels pouty
yuki (whispers): Ren. You're snoring.
ren (sleepy): I don't snore.
ren (thinks silently): ZZZZZ... ZZZZZZZ...
shot: close on yuki
impact on yuki
yuki (shouts, pouty): Ren!
shot: medium on both
ren (surprised): What? I was awake the whole time.
yuki (thinking): Then who was snoring?
ren (thinking): ...The owl?
owl flies in from left to left
pop: HOO! near owl
owl (pouty): Hoo! It was not me.
yuki (laughing): Even the owl knows.
ren (shy): Good night, everyone.
ending: Tag the one who "doesn't snore"!
