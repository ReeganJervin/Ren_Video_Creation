## How to add the calendar to your phone

On a computer: open calendar.google.com, then the gear (Settings), **Import & export**, **Import**, choose
`calendar\ren-yuki-week-2026-11-09.ics`, then **Import**. It then shows in Google Calendar on your phone, with reminders.
